FAQ

Common questions.

How do I access courses after payment? +

After submitting your payment via bKash or Nagad, our team verifies the transaction. Once approved, you get instant access through your dashboard.

What payment methods are available? +

We accept bKash and Nagad. After selecting a course, you'll see the payment number and can submit your transaction ID for verification.

How long do I have access? +

All course enrollments are valid for 365 days from the date of payment verification.

Can I download materials? +

Yes! Every course includes downloadable PDFs — lecture notes, formula sheets, and practice sets.

Is there a mobile app? +

Our platform is fully mobile-responsive. Access everything directly from your phone browser.
